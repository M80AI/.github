# M80AI

**Applied AI systems for business automation, agent workflows, and practical intelligence infrastructure.**

M80AI builds AI-powered products that help businesses capture leads, automate workflows, improve communication, and turn complex operations into usable software systems.

---

## What We Build

### Nextset AI
AI receptionist and lead intake infrastructure for service businesses.

- AI chat and phone intake
- Lead capture and routing
- CRM workflows
- Appointment handoff
- Small business automation

### Forge
AI-assisted application generation and rapid prototyping platform.

- Full-stack product scaffolding
- AI-assisted development workflows
- Prototype generation
- Deployment-focused builds

### Charlotte
Long-term AI memory and knowledge architecture.

- Persistent memory systems
- Retrieval workflows
- Human-AI interaction design
- Personal and organizational knowledge infrastructure

### Trinity Compression Engine
Experimental compression and data-efficiency research.

- Symbolic compression experiments
- AI-oriented encoding research
- Benchmarking and validation
- Efficient data representation
